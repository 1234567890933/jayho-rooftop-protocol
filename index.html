Five acts, five hands

  • I · The RunShake the phone to sprint, tap to jump the potholes. Touch mode: hold to sprint.
  • II · The ClimbTilt left and right to keep the box level. Touch mode: drag sideways.
  • III · The SetupTrace the fuse with your finger, then tap the spark in the green window.
  • IV · The LaunchHold to build pressure, release in the sweet spot, then tap at the apex.
  • V · The SkyDraw the letters. Embers follow your finger and catch fire.

Gyro look: once motion is allowed, tilting the phone also moves the camera — near layers slide further than far ones, so the street has depth. Sensitivity, invert-Y and recalibration live under Controls.

Motion sensing needs HTTPS and your permission. If it is unavailable the game switches to touch automatically — nothing is blocked.
